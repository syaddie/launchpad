New-tab dashboard. Every artboard is live: click events, complete tasks, type in notes, open drawers. Accent comparison first, then the nine screens, then the large and narrow sizes, then tokens.
Accent
1aSage, light (recommended)
1bSlate blue, light
1cTerracotta, light
Sage reads calmest against the warm neutrals and stays clearly apart from the clay-red used for overdue and exceptions. Terracotta sits too close to that warning colour, so “next event” and “overdue” start to look alike. Slate blue works but feels cooler than the rest of the palette.
